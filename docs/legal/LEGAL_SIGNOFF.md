# Legal Sign-Off Template

Before production launch, the following parties must sign.

## Parties

- Data Protection Officer (DPO).
- Legal Counsel (in-house or engaged).
- Engineering Lead.
- Security Lead.
- RRA sponsor.

- [ ] `docs/AUDIT_CONTRACT.md`
- [ ] `docs/legal/DPIA-template.md` (completed)

- [ ] `docs/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`
- [ ] `SECURITY.md`
