7. **Metrics emission** (B01 C-23): histograms / counters not yet wired. Need `prom-client` registration matching the existing metrics surface.
8. **Conjure-typed contract test** (D-03): generate a typed client from `types.ts` Zod schemas, exercise every endpoint at the wire level.
9. **Load test** (B01 C-24): k6 (or equivalent) measuring P50/P95/P99 against each operation. Requires k6 in the toolchain.
10. **Runbook entry** at `docs/B01.md` covering alerts, diagnosis, remediation.
