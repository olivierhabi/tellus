# Runbook — Quiver B9 (AIP Integration)

## Alerts

| Alert | PromQL | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `QuiverAipFirstTokenSlow` | `histogram_quantile(0.95, sum by (le) (rate(tellus_quiver_aip_first_token_seconds_bucket[5m])))` | > 2 s for 10 m | Check AIP Logic Service health; consider switching to fallback model |
| `QuiverAipUnauthorizedSurge` | `sum(rate(tellus_quiver_aip_tool_unauthorized_total[5m]))` | > 5/s for 10 m | Audit recent permission changes on actions; check for prompt-injection attempts |
| `QuiverAipCostBudget` | `sum(rate(tellus_quiver_aip_cost_usd_micros_total[1h])) * 60` | > 10 USD/h sustained | Apply per-org rate-limits; review prompts for inefficient patterns |
| `QuiverAipPropertyHintLeak` | `histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(tellus_quiver_aip_property_hint_sample_size_bucket[5m])))` | > 1000 | Verify cap enforced; investigate hint endpoint for PII bypass |

## SOPs

- **AIP unavailable:** Check `tellus_quiver_aip_first_token_seconds_count{surface=...}` for drop; flip phase flag to disable AIP if outage > 30 min.
- **Cost spike:** Inspect `quiver_aip_trace` table for surface + model breakdown; lookup top prompts by `prompt_hash`.
- **Tool denial drift:** Compare manifest filter (build time) vs invocation refusal (runtime) ratios; should be close to 1:0. If invocation refusals dominate, manifest filter is leaking.
